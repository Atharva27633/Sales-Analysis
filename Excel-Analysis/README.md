# Excel Analysis

## Overview
This folder contains the Excel workbook used for exploratory data analysis and business performance evaluation.

Microsoft Excel was used to analyze the cleaned retail sales dataset and identify important patterns related to sales, profit, and business performance.

## Analysis Performed
- Total sales, cost, and profit calculations.
- Profit margin analysis.
- Category-wise sales and profit analysis.
- Sub-category profitability analysis.
- Region-wise performance comparison.
- Customer segment analysis.
- Discount impact on profitability.

## Excel Features Used
- SUM, AVERAGE, COUNT, MIN, and MAX.
- IF and conditional calculations.
- SUMIFS, COUNTIFS, and AVERAGEIFS.
- Pivot Tables.
- Charts and data visualization.
- Sorting and filtering.

## Key Findings
- Technology was the highest-performing category in terms of sales and profit.
- The West region recorded the highest profit.
- Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies were among the loss-making sub-categories.
- Higher discounts negatively affected profitability.

## Purpose
The Excel analysis helped identify business trends and generate insights before moving to the visualization stage.
